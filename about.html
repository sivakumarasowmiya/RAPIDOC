About RapiDoc

RapiDoc is a healthcare platform conceptualized, designed, and developed solely by Sowmiya. With a passion for digital innovation in the healthcare domain, she has independently handled the entire development process—from front-end UI design to backend integration and deployment.

The platform aims to simplify the medical experience by offering features like online doctor appointments, teleconsultations, health record management, and more. Through RapiDoc, Sowmiya envisions a smarter healthcare future that is easily accessible, user-centric, and efficient.

This entire project stands as a testament to her dedication, coding expertise, and commitment to improving lives through technology.
